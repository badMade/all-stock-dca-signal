### Scope
Added focused unit tests for `dca_fileio.py` to cover previously untested lines and error branches. This increases the module's test coverage to 100%.

### AI Usage
I was utilized to identify the untested branches in `dca_fileio.py`, design the tests, implement them using `pytest` and `unittest.mock.patch`, and verify that the tests passed successfully against the pre-existing code.

### Assumptions & Validation
I assumed that the functions `iter_run_state_files_fast` and `iter_files_by_suffix_fast` should ignore `OSError` (such as permission denied) during directory scanning and just skip those directories. Validation involved injecting `OSError` via mock and asserting that execution continued and skipped the errored paths.

### Design Rationale
The tests isolate the external interactions (like the filesystem) using `tmp_path` and `patch`. They focus heavily on the untested branches such as `ValueError` handling in `order_csv_covers` and `OSError` ignoring in `_discard` and `os.scandir` usage.
